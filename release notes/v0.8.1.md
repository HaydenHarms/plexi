# Plexi 0.8.1

_Released November 10, 2026_

## New

- **Lock anything.** Ink, text and shapes can now be locked, not just pictures, and several at once: select them and press Ctrl+L or right-click → Lock. Locked things can't be picked up, moved, resized or erased. Right-click one to unlock it.
- **Print a picked area bigger, over several sheets.** After "Select area…" in Export, the new Size choice can fit the area on one sheet, scale it (10% to 1000%), or stretch it across a number of sheets. Each sheet prints its own part, ready to tape together.

## Changed

- **Turn from the corners.** The round handle above the selection is gone. Move the pointer just outside any corner of what's selected and it turns into a turning arrow; drag from there to rotate. Shift still snaps to 15° steps, and still turns a cropped photo inside its frame.

## Fixes

- Clicking or right-clicking the see-through part of a picture no longer picks the picture up. A mostly empty picture pasted onto the screen could be grabbed as a big invisible object anywhere inside its box.
