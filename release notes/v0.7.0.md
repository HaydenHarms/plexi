# Plexi 0.7.0

_Not released yet_

## New

- **Record to video.** Ctrl+Shift+R, or the new record button, records to an MP4 in your Videos folder. In the notebook it records the canvas; while drawing on screen it records the whole monitor with your ink on top. Switching between the two keeps recording into the same file. Press it again to stop. Windows only for now.
- **Scale text from its corners.** A text box now has corner handles too: dragging one scales the whole box, font size included. The side handles still set only the wrap width.
- **Print or export just a part.** Select something, then open Export (Ctrl+E or Ctrl+P): it starts on "Selected part", which covers just the box around your selection, whatever the sheet outlines are. Anything drawn across its edge is cut off there.

## Changed

- **Drawing on screen has the notebook's tools.** Its toolbar now has the menu, redo and all four shapes, alongside every tool.
- **One menu entry for export and print.** "Export or print…" replaces the separate Export and Print entries, which opened the same window.
- **Shift+click with the hand** adds things to the selection or takes them out, like the select tools.

## Fixes

- The handwriting window shows a stroke while you write it, not only once you lift the pen.
- Handwriting keeps its curves. Before, every stroke was simplified into a few straight lines. Letters you already wrote keep their straight lines; rewrite them to get the curves.
