# Plexi 0.7.0

_Released November 9, 2026_

## New

- **Record to video.** Ctrl+Shift+R, or the new record button, records to an MP4 in your Downloads folder. In the notebook it records the canvas; while drawing on screen it records the whole monitor with your ink on top. Switching between the two keeps recording into the same file. Press it again to stop. A dot with a short fading trail follows the mouse pointer in the video, so people can see where it is; its color, size and trail length are in Settings. Windows only for now.
- **Scale text from its corners.** A text box now has corner handles too: dragging one scales the whole box, font size included. The side handles still set only the wrap width.
- **Print or export just a part.** In Export (Ctrl+E or Ctrl+P), "Select area…" lets you drag a box on the canvas, whatever the sheet outlines are; anything drawn across its edge is cut off there. If you just took a snapshot, Export starts on that snapshot's area.
- **Snapshots of the screen.** The snapshot tool now works while drawing on screen: it copies what's on the screen under the box, with your ink on top. Before, it copied only the ink.
- **Drag to the edge to scroll.** Dragging a selection, a lasso or a box near the edge of the canvas scrolls the page that way, so you can reach things that are off screen.

## Changed

- **Drawing on screen has the notebook's tools.** Its toolbar now has the menu, redo and all four shapes, alongside every tool.
- **One menu entry for export and print.** "Export or print…" replaces the separate Export and Print entries, which opened the same window.
- **Shift+click with the hand** adds things to the selection or takes them out, like the select tools.
- **The lasso and rectangle select pick up anything they touch**, not only what they mostly surround.
- **Bigger close buttons.** The X on Export, Settings, Keyboard shortcuts and My handwriting reacts anywhere in a bigger area around it.

## Fixes

- The handwriting window shows a stroke while you write it, not only once you lift the pen.
- Handwriting keeps its curves. Before, every stroke was simplified into a few straight lines. Letters you already wrote keep their straight lines; rewrite them to get the curves.
- **Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V work inside Plexi again.** On Windows they never reached Plexi's own copy and paste, so Ctrl+V pasted whatever was on the system clipboard instead. A GIF copied in the notebook and pasted on screen came out as a still picture. Now Ctrl+V pastes what you copied in Plexi, animated GIFs and all, unless you've copied something elsewhere since.
