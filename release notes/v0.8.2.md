# Plexi 0.8.2

_Released November 10, 2026_

## New

- **Drop a PDF onto the canvas.** Drag a PDF file onto Plexi and its pages land where you drop them, one under the other, at the size they print (shrunk to fit when you're drawing on the screen). Each page is a picture you can write over, move, crop or turn. Up to 60 pages are brought in; one undo takes them all back out. Password-protected PDFs can't be opened yet.

## Fixes

- A picture bigger than the graphics card can hold could crash Plexi. It's now shown scaled down on screen and keeps its full detail in exports.
