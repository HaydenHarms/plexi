# Plexi 0.2.0

_Released November 5, 2026_

## New

- **Infinite canvas.** New notes start on an endless page. Sheet outlines appear wherever you draw, and they set the default PDF pagination. Older notes are migrated.
- **Paper colors** for each page, from swatches or a custom picker, with "use on every page". Ruled lines turn pale on dark paper.
- **Export dialog with a live preview** of every output page. You can pick the area (whole or trimmed), paper size, orientation, custom size, margins and pixel width, and shrink to one sheet or split across sheets. The CLI gains `--paper`, `--landscape`, `--tile`, `--trim` and `--margin`.
- **Images:** click or drag to pick one up, drag a corner to resize, and lock, unlock or reset its size. Locked images ignore the lasso, select-all and dragging.
- **Right-click menu** with lock, arrange, duplicate, delete, paper color and delete page.
- **Delete pages** from the page bar or the page list. You can undo it.

## Changed

- The mouse wheel zooms around the pointer. Trackpad scrolling and Shift+wheel pan.
- Right-drag pans the notebook; a right-click without dragging still opens the menu. When drawing on screen, right-drag still erases.
- Middle-drag erases with any tool, and a pen's barrel button keeps erasing.
- Fit view shows the whole width of an infinite page, and the window no longer opens under the taskbar.
