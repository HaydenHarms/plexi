# Plexi 0.8.4

_Released November 17, 2026_

## New

- **Arrow keys on the canvas.** With something selected, the arrow keys move it a little at a time (hold Shift for bigger steps), and one undo puts it all back. With nothing selected, they scroll the canvas.
- **Export just the selection.** Right-click what you've selected and choose **Export selection…** to save it as a PNG the size of the selection, without anything else that overlaps it. Untick **Only what's selected** to include everything in that area.
- **Save a picture as it was.** Right-click an image and choose **Save picture…** to save the original file you brought in.

## Fixes

- Exporting a picked area as an image (PNG, JPEG, …) now gives a picture of just that area, not a whole sheet of paper with the area on it.
