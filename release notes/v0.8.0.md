# Plexi 0.8.0

_Released November 9, 2026_

## New

- **Turn anything.** Select something and drag the round handle above it to rotate it: shapes, text, pictures, ink, or several at once. Hold Shift to snap to 15° steps. Turned things stay turned in every export.
- **Straighten a cropped photo.** Shift+drag the rotate handle on a cropped photo to turn the photo inside its frame while the frame stays put. It settles on straight when you get close.
- **Resize shapes.** Drag the ends of a line or arrow, or the corners of a rectangle or ellipse. Shift snaps, like when drawing them.
- **Groups.** Right-click → Group, or Ctrl+G, joins what's selected so it's picked up and moved as one. Ctrl+Shift+G or Ungroup splits it again.
- **Remove a picture's background.** Right-click a picture → Remove background makes the plain color around it (a white page behind a logo, say) see-through. It works on plain backgrounds, not busy photos.
- **Place a picked area on the sheet.** After "Select area…" in Export, drag the area in the preview to put it anywhere on the paper.
- **Several handwriting fonts.** The handwriting window can hold as many fonts as you like, each with its own name. Renaming one carries text already set in it along.
- **Save your handwriting as a font file.** "Export .ttf…" in the handwriting window saves a TrueType font you can install or use in other programs.
- **Keyboard shortcuts for text.** F2 edits the selected text box; Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I and Ctrl+U toggle bold, italic and underline.

## Changed

- **Text box corners resize freely.** Dragging a corner sideways sets the width the text wraps to; up and down sets the font size. Hold Shift for the old behavior, scaling both together.
- **Each selected thing gets its own outline** instead of one big box around everything. A group gets one outline.

## Fixes

- Messages at the bottom of the window (like "Saved …") no longer stack their words one per line after a shorter message.
