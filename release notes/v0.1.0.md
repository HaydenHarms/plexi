# Plexi 0.1.0

_Released November 5, 2026_

The first release: a light ink notebook for Windows that can also turn your whole screen into a sheet of glass you draw on.

## Highlights

- **Draw on screen.** Press Ctrl+Alt+D anywhere and a transparent, full-screen overlay lets you write over any app. Esc switches to click-through, so your ink stays on top while you keep working underneath. Global hotkeys, a tray icon and screen snapshots come with it.
- **Pressure-sensitive ink** with light smoothing that steadies slow strokes without rounding off fast writing.
- **Notebook tools:** pages, a floating toolbar, pen, highlighter, eraser, lasso, text and shapes, undo, autosave and a command palette (Ctrl+K).
- **Exports** to PDF, PNG, JPEG, WebP, BMP, TIFF, SVG, HTML, Markdown, plain text, Excalidraw, draw.io, InkML and JSON.

## Downloads

An Inno Setup installer, an all-users MSI, and portable builds (exe and zip).
